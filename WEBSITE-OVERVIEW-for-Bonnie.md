# Your Website — A Simple Overview

*Hearten Home Interiors · prepared for Bonnie*

---

## Your five pages

- **Home** — Your welcome. It introduces who you are, shares a glimpse of your story, and gives every visitor a clear path to reach out.
- **About** — Your thirty years and your design philosophy, so a visitor feels they know you before they ever call.
- **Services** — What you offer (renovation, full-service design, styling, holiday readiness) and how you work alongside a client.
- **Portfolio** — Your beloved homes. Each project gets its own page with photos and the story behind it. This is the page that turns a curious visitor into a phone call.
- **Contact** — A simple form. When someone fills it out, the inquiry comes straight to you.

---

## Built to help the right people find you

A beautiful website only matters if the right people see it. Here is what is working quietly in the background:

- **You'll show up in search.** Every page is set up so Google understands you are a Dallas/Fort Worth interior designer and shows you to people searching for exactly that. Your business details are organized the way Google prefers, which helps you appear in local results and on the map.
- **It looks professional everywhere it's shared.** When you or a happy client shares your link in a text or on social media, it shows your name, a description, and a photo — not a bare web address.
- **It's fast.** Every photo is optimized automatically, so your pages load quickly. Visitors stay longer, and Google rewards it.
- **No inquiry slips through.** The contact form delivers straight to your inbox, and the site is built to stay online reliably.

---

## The whole site is yours to update

You can change the words and photos anywhere on the site yourself, any time, through one simple login at **heartenhome.com/admin**. You only ever fill in forms and swap photos, so the design stays perfect and you cannot break anything.

**To edit a page** (Home, About, Services, Contact): open **Website Pages**, pick the page, change any text or photo, and click **Publish.**

**To add a portfolio project:** open **Portfolio Projects**, click **New Project**, fill in the details and photos, and click **Publish.**

Changes appear on the site a minute or two later. Photos can come straight from your phone; they're resized for you automatically. *(A one-page printed guide is included.)*

## Your next steps for a strong online presence

A few things will make the biggest difference:

1. **Add your real projects.** The portfolio is the heart of the site. Aim for your best 6–10 homes to start. *(The two projects shown right now are just examples to demonstrate how it works.)*
2. **Send a few service photos.** The Services page has space for four images — one each for renovation, design, styling, and holiday readiness.
3. **Add your phone number,** if you'd like calls, so it can appear on the site and in Google.
4. **Set up your Google Business Profile.** For a local designer this is one of the highest-impact things you can do; it places you directly on Google Maps and in local search. *(Happy to set this up with you.)*
5. **Keep adding projects over time.** Every new project becomes a new page for Google to find, which steadily grows how often you turn up in search.
