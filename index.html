Meet Bonnie

For thirty years, Bonnie Roden was the friend everyone called — the one who could walk into a home and see what it was trying to become.

In 2024, she finally put her name on a door. Hearten Home Interiors is the business she spent three decades building without knowing it, and her goal is as simple as it is rare: to make you feel better about your home…and to truly love it. For you to have an atmosphere that refreshes and inspires you…not just because it's beautiful, but because it's a reflection of you. She designs with purpose: thoughtful spaces that work beautifully, live well, and last.
