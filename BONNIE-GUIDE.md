# How to Update Your Website

You can change the words and photos anywhere on your site, anytime, from your
computer or phone. You only ever type into boxes and add photos, so you can relax:
**you cannot break the design.** If something ever looks wrong, you can change it
right back.

## 1. Log in
1. Go to **heartenhome.com/admin**
2. Enter your email and password.
3. You'll see two buttons: **Website Pages** and **Portfolio Projects.**

## 2. Change words or photos on a page
1. Click **Website Pages.**
2. Click the page you want: **Home, About, Services,** or **Contact.**
3. You'll see every piece of text and every photo on that page, each in its own
   box, labeled with what it currently says.
4. Type your change into any box. To change a photo, click it, then pick a new one
   from your computer or phone.
5. Click the green **Publish** button at the top right, then **Publish now.**

## 3. Add a new portfolio project
1. Click **Portfolio Projects.**
2. Click **New Project** at the top.
3. Fill in the boxes: project name, service type (pick from the list), location,
   year (optional), a short description, your cover photo, and any extra photos.
4. Click the green **Publish** button, then **Publish now.**

## 4. Edit or remove a project
1. Click **Portfolio Projects.**
2. Click the project you want.
3. Change anything, then **Publish** again. Or click **Delete entry** at the top
   to remove it.

## Three things to remember
1. **Saving is not the same as publishing.** While you work, you may see the words
   "Changes saved." That only means your work is held safely. It does **not** go on
   your website until you click the green **Publish** button.
2. **Changes take about a minute.** After you publish, wait a minute or two, then
   refresh your actual website (not this editor) to see them.
3. **Photos are easy.** Add any photo straight from your phone or camera. The site
   shrinks it for you automatically, so you never have to think about size.

## A few notes
- A few large decorative headlines are fixed in place so their special styling
  always looks perfect. Everything else is yours to change.
- You truly cannot break anything. If you change something by mistake, just open it
  again, fix it, and publish.
- Stuck on anything at all? Reach out to Jayce and he'll sort it out quickly.
